The flow under test is: every built-in Workbench route and creation backend → every visible and revealable task state → controls remain consistently grouped, aligned, contained, and usable.

## Contract and method

The existing shared role tokens define a 4 px association between a field label and its control, 8 px between related controls and actions, and 12 px between successive fields. The coordinator's provisional grouping contract adds 12 px between affected content and its actions, 16 px between subgroups, and 24 px between unrelated topics or sections. Intentional deviations will be recorded with their purpose. Desktop control height is 36 px and mobile touch height is at least 44 px; inner input and outer action wrappers are measured separately.

Each capture records actual bounding rectangles for every visible and hidden control, outer action/control families, text line boxes, explicit labels, CSS padding/margins/borders, flex/grid alignment, direct sibling edge gaps, section content, and document/region overflow. DOM controls hidden until a later state remain in the inventory. Every capture has a screenshot and JSON artifact under `/tmp/rdf4j7-workbench-spacing-20260929/browser`. Gap conclusions use visible edges, including margins and padding, rather than CSS `gap` alone. Wrapped groups and deliberate distributed alignment are analyzed separately.

Baseline tracked status was clean at branch `GH-6071-workbench-redesign`, commit `d6e9ea70a547b48942106cec515cdb6839c21728`. Coordinator owns all production changes, builds, and server lifecycle. This worker owns only the two spacing specifications and this report.

## Intentional spacing differences

- The main shell uses 24 px desktop gutters, 12 px mobile gutters, 20 px desktop card padding and 16 px mobile card padding. Those are outer layout roles, not control gaps.
- Two-column normal forms use 12 px row rhythm and 16 px column separation. Label/helper association is the smaller 4 px role.
- Custom disclosure content has a 12 px inset plus border, an 8 px trigger/panel anchor gap, and deliberate indentation where the parent specifies it. Native Details, table headers, data records, and diagram/tree children have their own nested-content roles.
- The memory Create form grows from roughly 409 px to 642 px when optional content appears; its left edge stays aligned. Fit-content width growth is intentional. The defects are grouping and compound gaps, not a requirement to make all forms/fields equal width.
- Plain desktop controls are 36 px. Normal mobile controls are at least 44 px; decorated and compare controls can have a 46 px outer hitbox around a 44 px inner control. The outer target is the size contract.
- Check/radio glyphs, dense navigation/data-tree rows, and vendor editor icon controls are compact visual families. Their associated label/row is inspected separately from the glyph. They are not compared to the padding of a full form action.
- Compact JSON branches and leaf spacers intentionally own 24 px boxes with zero inset. Fixed compare close keeps its existing square hit area and a centered 16 px SVG with zero inset.
- Space-between headers deliberately distribute spare space between identity/status and utility actions. Large distributed gaps are not normalized to 8 px; their minimum related-item gap, vertical alignment and outer edge containment are checked instead.
- Wide RDF tables and explanation/code surfaces may scroll inside bounded regions. A contained scrollable value is different from the clipped Share popup or an unbounded saved heading.
- Saved read-only source follows its content until the existing 55vh scroll cap. The long-source assertion is `min(rendered content height, computed scroll max-height) + borders`, with a public CodeMirror scroll-to-bottom reachability check. The one-line equality remains strict; the original 302-versus-31 px RED is preserved.
- Compact JSON branches and leaf spacers own 24×24 px boxes. Native triangle text uses the existing 13 px/15.6 px line-height font. Its Range center is −0.796875 px vertically and −0.0078125 px horizontally in Chromium; an explicitly flex-centered diagnostic clone produces the identical Range metrics. This is native font ascent/leading rather than a content alignment defect; no optical pixel offset is introduced. Actual Range bounds are recorded with compact geometry (`component-edge-geometry.json`, `component-typography-counterfactual.json`).
