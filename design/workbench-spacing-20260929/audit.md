## Semantic spacing contract

The distance between a label or helper and its control conveys association. Neighboring controls/actions belong to a group. A larger distance separates a subgroup, and a still larger distance separates independent topics or sections. The scale is 4px for label/helper association, 8px for related controls, 12px for successive fields and affected content to actions, 16px for subgroups, and 24px for separate topics. A layout owner assigns the distance once; native control margins and nested padding must not add an accidental second distance.

All distances are measured between rendered edges, with padding, border, margin, grid/flex gap, and distributed alignment recorded separately. A CSS `gap` token by itself does not establish the visible distance: margins, padding, and alignment can add to it, as explained by [MDN's gap reference](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Reference/Properties/gap). Controls should be positioned and grouped with the content or actions they affect, consistent with [W3C's guidance on related controls](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/patterns/o1p06-control-actions/).

Equivalent control families should use the same padding and outer-margin ownership. Composite controls use their outer hit area for physical measurement; an inner button's zero padding may be intentional if its wrapper owns the inset. Compact icon-only editor controls, ordinary text actions, data cells, field labels, and topic sections are different roles, and any different sizing must be justified explicitly. The current baseline declares 36px desktop and 44px touch-height tokens; rendered heights are measured and preserved rather than assumed.
