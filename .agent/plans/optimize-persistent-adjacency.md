## Surprises & Discoveries

Initial source inspection finds that logical-row scans repeatedly open page and delta cursors even when many rows share a physical page. Raw native-transaction scans do not use the snapshot decoder cache, so one shared page can be decoded repeatedly. Successful writes also probe the outgoing delta/base more than once and perform many separate metadata get/put operations. Bulk paths still prepare logical permutation encodings that are later discarded. These are hypotheses until recorded work counts and benchmark measurements confirm their cost.
